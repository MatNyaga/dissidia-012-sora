## In plain words (for Mat)
- Every menu that shows a fighter's picture as "base + fighter number − 1" had a little Sora rule written into it
  ("if it is fighter 33, use picture 245"). 15 such places (party screens, Quick Battle setup banner, Adjust Level,
  the party edit strips, the party list) now read the picture number from one small table, and the party frame and
  the setup frame band from two more tables. The Characters panel (□) reads its sprite from a fourth table.
- The tables hold the original numbers for every vanilla fighter and Sora's numbers for Sora. The installer will write
  a second fighter's numbers the same way, without new code.
- Side by side with the reference disc, the same screens look the same (Sora's face, name plate and frame; Cloud,
  Prishe, Lightning unchanged), and the menus are as fast as before.
